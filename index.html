BEYOND THE VEIL

How to Play

Explore the world, discover secrets, collect powerful items and find new abilities.

A / D or ← / → — Move

W / Space / ↑ — Jump

ESC — Pause

Goal

Find the Crown and bring it to the throne at the end of the game.

Press ENTER to start

DOUBLE JUMP UNLOCKED

You found a mysterious gemstone.

You can now jump a second time while you are in the air.

Press ENTER or SPACE to continue

PAUSED
